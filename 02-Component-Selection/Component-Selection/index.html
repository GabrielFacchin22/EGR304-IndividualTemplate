    title: Component Selection

    Component Selection – GripRx Motor Board

    Course: EGR 304 – Embedded Systems Design
    Team: 103 – GripRx
    Project: Motor Board

    Overview

    The purpose of this assignment is to evaluate and compare major electronic components for the GripRx Motor Board. The board uses a PIC18F57Q43 microcontroller to control a DC gear motor through an H-bridge motor driver.

    The selection process considers electrical compatibility, performance, cost, power requirements, and ease of integration.

    The major components evaluated are:

    1. DC Gear Motor
    2. H-Bridge Motor Driver
    3. 5V Voltage Regulator
    4. 9V Power Supply

    Preliminary Choice: Option 1 – DFRobot FIT0492-A Metal DC Gear Motor.

    Rationale: The DFRobot FIT0492-A is the preferred preliminary choice because it provides a useful combination of low output speed, substantial rated torque, and low cost. Its rated torque of 9 kg-cm and rated current of 0.68A make it a promising candidate for the GripRx gripping mechanism. The 50 RPM no-load speed may also help provide controlled opening and closing movement. Compared with the Pololu alternatives, it offers an attractive balance between torque and cost. However, the motor is rated for 12V, while the current block diagram specifies a 9V power supply. The power supply selection should therefore be reconsidered. Additionally, the motor has a stall current of 2.19A, which exceeds the currently proposed 1A motor-driver current limit. The team must evaluate the motor driver's current-limit setting and confirm that the motor can produce sufficient gripping force without overheating or stalling. The final motor selection will be confirmed after the mechanical design is completed and the required gripping torque is calculated or measured.

    Sources: - DFRobot FIT0492-A Manufacturer Specifications - Pololu 50:1 Metal Gearmotor Specifications - Pololu 150:1 Micro Metal Gearmotor Listing
